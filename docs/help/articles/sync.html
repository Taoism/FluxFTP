Synchronize folders

Open Tools → Synchronize Folders to compare two folders and preview proposed actions. Review the action for every row, especially overwrites and destination-only deletions, before approving the plan. Approved work is placed in the queue and can be started when ready.

Checksum comparison requires a common supported native hash. Saved baselines are snapshots of a scan; they do not prove a later transfer completed. Changes to either side after preview can make the plan stale. Re-scan and review it before applying if inputs have changed.
