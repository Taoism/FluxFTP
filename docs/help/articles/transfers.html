Browse and transfer files

Navigate folders in either pane. Select one or more files and folders to queue transfers. FluxFTP supports dragging selected items between panes when the source and destination accept the operation; you can also use the transfer commands and context menus.

Queued work is visible in the transfer queue. You can hold selected jobs, adjust supported options, retry failed jobs, and recover unfinished queue entries. Conflict prompts let you choose how an existing destination file is handled. Available resume options depend on the protocol and server.

Transfer direction

Local-to-remote copies upload. Remote-to-local copies download. Remote-to-remote copies may use direct FXP or relay through this computer, depending on the sites and route. See Remote-to-remote transfers.

Use Transfer History for completed outcomes and verification evidence. Historical exports report recorded checks; they do not reread the current files.
